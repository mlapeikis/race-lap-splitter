Race Lap Splitter

Split a Strava ride into laps at the start/finish, sort each lap onto its route, and compare lap times, climbing and heart rate route by route.

  1. Export from Strava. On strava.com (the website, not the app), open your race ride, click the ⋯ menu and choose Export GPX.
  2. Drop the file here. Add several riders' files at once if you collected them. Files stay in your browser.
  3. Check the gate and routes. Click the map to move the start/finish, rename routes, fix any lap's route, then download.
